💡 The Heavy-Tail Secret:

Gaussian in high-D: $p_{j|i} \propto e^{-\|x_i-x_j\|^2 / 2\sigma^2}$
Student-t in 2D: $q_{ij} \propto (1 + \|y_i-y_j\|^2)^{-1}$
The inverse-square heavy tail pushes moderately dissimilar points far apart, solving the Crowding Problem.

⚠️ Interpretation Caution:

Never interpret cluster distances in t-SNE as physical metric distances. Cluster size in t-SNE expands or shrinks depending on density; use it strictly for finding topology and discrete groupings.
